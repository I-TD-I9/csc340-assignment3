Gilgamesh

Profile Summary

  • Universe: Epic of Gilgamesh
  • Role: King of Uruk
  • Power Level: Two-thirds divine
  • Species: Demigod
  • First Appearance: Tablet I

Backstory

Gilgamesh begins as a proud ruler of Uruk, unmatched in strength and ambition. His friendship with Enkidu shapes his journey toward wisdom.

Abilities

Renowned for immense strength and courage, he leads expeditions against Humbaba and the Bull of Heaven.

Personality Traits

Proud, determined, and reflective in his search for meaning.
